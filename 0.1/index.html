    Welcome to Fivemon

    Fivemon is a comprehensive mod/resource set for FiveM, featuring a custom framework at its core called uri-core. This state-of-the-art framework is highly optimized and offers a wide array of customization options, easy translation, and seamless content addition through simple files or even its own custom language, .uri. This language allows developers to create advanced NPCs, quests, and logic-rich gameplay elements to enhance the player experience. Fivemon is also equipped with a powerful Monster mechanics addon, supporting an advanced battle simulator.

    In the Fivemon Wiki, you'll be able to dive into learning .uri starting with the introduction. In guides, you can explore various topics to help you learn or better understand different aspects of the framework. In the core and monsters sections, you'll find up to four main categories: resources, extensions, and utilities. In resources, you'll have access to several repositories containing server content, such as items, Monster, abilities, and more. The extensions section provides additional functionalities for .uri, including new actions and constructors. Finally, in utilities, you'll find commands, configuration files, and control schemes to manage and customize your experience.

    Explore this page to discover all the exciting features Fivemon has to offer.

    Monster Team

    Each player has access to an advanced Monster team, allowing them to easily view and manage their team. The interface is user-friendly, offering a clear view of each Monster's stats, abilities, and moves.

    Fivemon includes all the essential mechanics needed for modern Monster gameplay, including stats like EVs, IVs, natures, and abilities. Monster can evolve, level up, and participate in complex battles, all within a flexible and immersive system.

    Monster PC

    Players can also utilize a fully configurable PC system with filters to help organize and search for Monster. For example, you can filter Monster based on whether they can learn a specific TM, change box colors, and much more.

    Monster Interactions

    Players can carry their Monster and interact with them organically by throwing them out, recalling them, or having them follow in a natural, fluid manner.

    Advanced Spawn System

    Fivemon's spawn system is highly configurable, determining which Monster appear in each area based on biomes, time of day, weather conditions, and rarity tiers. This makes rare Monster encounters even more special and exciting.

    Monsterdex

    Players have access to a Monsterdex, allowing them to track their progress as trainers. They can use the Monsterdex to capture information using the in-game camera or their Monster list. The Monsterdex provides valuable insights into their journey.

    Skins for Monster

    Fivemon introduces a unique system for custom Monster skins, allowing players to customize their Monster and enhance their connection with their team. This feature adds a personal touch, making the gameplay experience even more rewarding.

    Flexible Battle System

    The battle system in Fivemon is extremely flexible, allowing for a variety of battle formats. You can have battles with up to four teams, and each team can have up to six Monster. This means you can create battles like 2v2, 3v3, or even free-for-alls like 1v1v1v1. The possibilities are endless. NPCs, wild Monster, mission Monster, or players can all participate in battles.

    The battle interface is user-friendly, showing all the relevant information needed to manage the battle. Additionally, spectators can watch battles by simply standing near them, encouraging player interaction.

    Gym Badges

    Fivemon includes a Gym and Badge system, where players can take on Gym Leaders and collect badges as they work towards becoming the Champion. This structured challenge adds a competitive layer to the experience, motivating players to rise through the ranks.

    Customization for Playmakers

    Fivemon offers a wide range of commands for playmakers to customize the experience. Whether it’s creating custom Monster, designing new items like custom potions or Monster balls, or setting up unique zones that only players with specific qualifications can access, the possibilities are endless.

    Custom Zones

    You can create zones that only trainers with certain achievements or levels can enter. For example, you might have a special area for players above level 50 where rare and powerful Monster appear.

    Custom Achievements

    You can also define achievements for players, such as defeating 50 wild Monster or completing the Monsterdex. These achievements can be tied to zones, creating a progression system that rewards dedicated players.

    Custom Content

    Using .uri, you can create custom NPCs with personalized dialogues and behaviors. For instance, you could design an evil duo that blocks access to a specific area until they are defeated in a 2v2 battle. You can even create raid battles with Monster levels over 1000, encouraging community teamwork to achieve a shared goal.

    Monster Skills

    In Fivemon, your Monster aren't just battle companions—they can also assist you in exploring the world in exciting ways. You can fly through the skies on the back of a flying-type Monster, surf across the water, or even dive into the depths with a water-type. Additionally, with certain Monster have the ability to ride them or you can carry them.

    Core Framework Features

    Beyond the Monster content, uri-core provides a powerful framework with features such as:

    • A custom loading screen that displays user information.
    • A custom character loading system.
    • An advanced inventory system with window-based navigation.
    • A pause/personal menu that centralizes everything a player needs to navigate the world easily. Organizations, quests, quest group, settings...

    Endless Possibilities

    Fivemon gives you all the tools to create your own world. Whether you want to develop an advanced Monster ecosystem, build a thriving player community, or design intricate NPC interactions, Fivemon is here to help you achieve it. The only limit is your imagination.
